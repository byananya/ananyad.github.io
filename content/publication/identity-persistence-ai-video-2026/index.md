abstract: 'AI-generated outbound video is increasingly used for founder-led engagement, but generated sequences can drift from a sender''s actual likeness and presentation across a touch sequence, undermining recipient trust. We introduce the Sequence Persistence Score (SPS), a standardized evaluation framework for measuring identity consistency across AI-generated video sequences. SPS combines face persistence — cosine similarity between face embeddings extracted with InsightFace buffalo_l — and style persistence — consistency of background, lighting, and colour-grade features across outputs — into a weighted pass/fail metric (0.60 face / 0.40 style, pass threshold 0.70). We validate the framework on a consented founder-identity dataset and release the evaluation code, sample data, and a reproducible notebook; multi-subject calibration is left to future work.'
